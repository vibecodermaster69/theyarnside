# THE YARN SIDE — STRICT BRAND GUIDELINES

**Brand:** THE YARN SIDE  
**Tagline:** *May the yarn be with you.*  
**Direction:** Polished Handmade Boutique  
**Status:** LOCKED / APPROVED

> This document is the source of truth for all design, development, social, marketplace, and packaging work. Deviations require explicit owner approval.

## 1. Brand character
Must feel: **polished, warm, handmade, elegant, boutique, calm, trustworthy, modern**.

Must never feel: childish, kawaii-heavy, cartoonish, cluttered, neon, generic marketplace, discount-led, franchise-parody, or craft-fair rustic.

## 2. Locked naming
Use exactly **THE YARN SIDE**.  
Use exactly **May the yarn be with you.**

Do not rewrite the tagline or imitate any film/franchise visual identity.

## 3. Colors
- Cocoa `#4B3A32` — primary typography / marks
- Coral `#E07A69` — primary accent / CTA
- Blush `#F2C6B9` — supporting accent
- Sage `#8FA293` — secondary accent / calm surfaces
- Warm Cream `#F7F2EA` — main background
- White `#FFFFFF` — cards / product surfaces

Recommended balance: 45–60% Cream/White, 20–30% Cocoa, 10–15% Coral, 5–10% Sage, under 8% Blush.

**Forbidden:** neon, rainbow gradients, default black replacing Cocoa, random recolored logos, new primary colors without approval.

## 4. Logo hierarchy
Use assets from `01_LOGOS/`.

- **Primary:** hero, About, formal brand use
- **Horizontal/no-tagline:** navigation, banners, compact headers
- **Signature:** packaging, inserts, stationery
- **Round seal:** stickers / seals
- **Monogram / brand mark:** favicon, DP, watermark, small labels

Never stretch, skew, redraw, recolor, add shadows/glows, add extra flowers, or recreate the wordmark with random fonts.

Minimum digital widths:
- Primary: 420px
- Horizontal: 240px
- Signature: 300px
- Monogram: 32px

## 5. Typography
**Headings:** Playfair Display.  
Acceptable close alternative: Cormorant Garamond.  
**Body:** Lora.  
**UI fallback:** Lato.  
**Accent script:** decorative only.

Never use script for body copy, prices, buttons, forms, navigation, or accessibility-critical text.  
Never use more than two active type families on a page excluding logo artwork.

## 6. Photography
Use natural/diffused light, cream/linen/wood surfaces, visible stitch texture, subtle props, maker/process imagery, and clean lifestyle scenes.

Avoid harsh flash, clutter, heavy filters, oversaturation, unrelated stock images, or props that compete with the crochet product.

## 9. Instagram
Use `03_INSTAGRAM/`.

Recommended mix:
- 50% product/lifestyle
- 25% process/BTS
- 15% branded education
- 10% review/announcement

Profile image: monogram or simplified round mark only.  
Reel cover: 3–6 words maximum.  
Do not turn the feed into a wall of text templates.

## 10. WhatsApp
Use `04_WHATSAPP/`.

Profile: simplified mark.  
Catalog: product first, branding second.  
Status: concise, high-contrast, readable on mobile.  
Do not over-design functional WhatsApp content.

## 11. Packaging
Use `05_PACKAGING/`.

Preferred materials: kraft, cream uncoated stock, natural tissue, neutral ribbon.

Preferred print order:
1. Cocoa one-color stamp/seal
2. Coral + Cocoa
3. Full color only where print quality supports it

Do not force full-color floral artwork onto every package.

## 12. Amazon / marketplace
Primary product image must follow marketplace image rules and prioritize product clarity. Use branding mainly on secondary images, storefront banners, packaging, maker story, and feature cards.

## 13. Iconography
Thin, simple, rounded, restrained. Use Cocoa/Coral/Sage.  
No emoji UI, glossy 3D icons, corporate blue packs, or mixed stroke styles.

## 14. Shapes and effects
Allowed: soft rounded cards, pill buttons, circles, organic curves, restrained yarn lines.  
Avoid: aggressive polygons, heavy glassmorphism, neon glow, bevels, metallic effects, oversized gradients.

## 15. Motion
Allowed: 150–250ms fades, soft hover lift, tiny image zoom, yarn-line draw.  
Forbidden: bouncing logos, spinning yarn balls, cursor trails, scroll hijacking, distracting loops.

## 16. Tone
Warm, human, thoughtful, lightly playful, confident, calm.

Approved-style phrases:
- `Fresh from the Yarn Side.`
- `Made one loop at a time.`
- `Got something cozy in mind?`
- `Behind the loops.`
- `Stay on the Yarn Side.`

Avoid ALL-CAPS sales spam, fake urgency, exaggerated luxury language, baby-talk, and excessive exclamation marks.

## 17. PNG vs JPEG
PNG: transparent logo, overlays, icons, favicon, web decoration, packaging artwork.  
JPEG: social posts, WhatsApp graphics, mockups, previews.

Do not use JPEG when transparency is required.

## 18. Forbidden brand changes
Without explicit approval, never:
- redesign the logo
- change the tagline
- replace yarn ball/crochet-hook language
- add a mascot
- switch to dark theme
- introduce neon/gradient branding
- use franchise graphics/type
- make the identity childish
- add a new primary color
- publish AI-generated alternate logos as official marks
